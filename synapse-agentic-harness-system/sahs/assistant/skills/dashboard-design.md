# Dashboard design

One question per panel; a panel that needs a paragraph is two
panels.

- Lead with the number that decides something (a KPI tile), then
  the trend that explains it (line), then the comparison that
  locates it (bar), then the detail table for the skeptic.
- Every tile that shows a number carries its status and meridian
  line — the schema enforces it; design for it: the disclosure is a
  caption, not an apology.
- Chart choice: change over time → line; comparison across few
  categories → bar; relationship → scatter; share of whole at one
  moment → bar over pie, always.
- Composed tiles wear the EXPLORATORY watermark until their check
  fact is cited; place them below certified tiles, never beside
  them as equals.
- Titles say the finding, not the metric ("CA spend fell 8% in
  March", not "Spend by month").
- Exports carry the provenance footer; leave room for it.
