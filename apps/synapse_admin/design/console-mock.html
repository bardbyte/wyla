Design principles

Four rules generate every decision above. If a future screen violates one, the screen is wrong.

01The answer leads

A VP reads only the bold sentence. Everything else — method, citations, trace — is one gesture away, never in the way.

02Latency is narrated

Gemini thinking is slow. A spinner says “broken”; a trace that says “dry-running the query…” says expert at work. The rail is the loading state.

03Refusal is a feature

The cm11 block renders as a designed safety beat with the rule cited and a compliant path offered — the strongest trust moment in the room.

04Provenance is pigment

The only semantic colors in the app are the evidence tiers. If something is green, it's grounded — nowhere else is green allowed to mean anything.

The persona lens

One stream, two renderings — flip the toggle above to feel it. Executive hides the trace rail, the “How I got there” expanders, and sandbox code (the result stays in the answer); the gate compresses to its resolved line. Analyst shows everything. Citations, governance lines, and status stamps survive both lenses — trust is never optional.
