# Industry Landscape: Semantic Layers and the Interfaces Built on Them (2025–2026)

**Status:** research report, compiled 2026-07-07. Companion to `docs/SEMANTIC_LAYER_BLUEPRINT.md`.
**Method:** web research over primary sources (vendor docs, launch posts, engineering blogs, press releases, arXiv), with load-bearing claims (accuracy numbers, GA dates, M&A) verified against at least one primary or two independent secondary sources. Every substantive claim carries a link. Accuracy figures are flagged as vendor-reported unless independently published.

---

## Executive Summary

**The five defining industry patterns of 2025–2026:**

1. **Semantic grounding is sold with an accuracy delta.** Every serious vendor now justifies its semantic layer with a "with vs. without" NL→SQL benchmark: Snowflake reports GPT-4o alone at 51% on real BI questions vs. 90%+ for Cortex Analyst on semantic models ([Snowflake engineering blog](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/cortex-analyst-text-to-sql-accuracy-bi/)); AtScale reports 92.5% vs. 20% on TPC-DS ([AtScale/Businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240808651958/en/AtScale-Unveils-Breakthrough-in-Natural-Language-Processing-with-Semantic-Layer-and-Generative-AI)); data.world's peer-reviewed benchmark shows 16.7% → 54.2% with a knowledge graph ([arXiv:2311.07509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07509)); an independent 2026 paired benchmark shows +17–23pp across three frontier models ([arXiv:2604.25149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25149)). "Trusted data + LLM" is the universal pitch; the semantic layer is now marketed primarily as *the thing that makes AI not lie*.
2. **The verified-answer escrow is THE trust mechanism.** The industry's convergent answer to "how do users trust the bot" is a human-blessed question→query repository surfaced as a binary badge: Snowflake's Verified Query Repository ([docs](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-analyst/verified-query-repository)), Databricks Genie's Trusted Assets ([docs](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/trusted-assets)), Power BI's Verified Answers ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/copilot-prepare-data-ai-verified-answers)), Amazon Q's verified questions ([AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/business-intelligence/curate-topics-in-amazon-q-in-quicksight-to-derive-maximum-value-and-improve-user-experience/)), ThoughtSpot's coaching loop ([docs](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotter-coach)). Trust is asset-level and binary — verified or not — never per-fact, never calibrated.
3. **MCP-ification of the whole stack.** Between April 2025 (dbt's MCP server, [launch post](https://docs.getdbt.com/blog/introducing-dbt-mcp-server)) and mid-2026, effectively every semantic layer, warehouse, and catalog shipped an MCP server: Snowflake managed MCP (GA [Nov 4, 2025](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/en/release-notes/2025/other/2025-11-04-cortex-agents-mcp)), Databricks managed MCP ([blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/announcing-managed-mcp-servers-unity-catalog-and-mosaic-ai-integration)), Cube, Honeydew, Wren Engine, Malloy Publisher, Atlan, Alation, Collibra, Select Star, OpenMetadata, DataHub. The semantic layer's new primary consumer is an agent, and agent governance = RBAC pass-through + post-hoc audit logs — **not** pre-execution human approval.
4. **The "context layer" land-grab and the consolidation wave.** Metadata vendors rebranded as context ("Atlan — The Context Layer for AI", DataHub "The Context Platform", OpenMetadata "the open context layer", Databricks' Genie Ontology "context layer"), and the platforms bought the category: Select Star→Snowflake (Nov 2025), data.world→ServiceNow (May 2025), Numbers Station→Alation (May 2025), Seek AI→IBM (June 2025), Secoda→Atlassian, Metaphor→KPMG (Apr 2025), plus the dbt+Fivetran merger (announced Oct 13, 2025; closed June 1, 2026) pitched explicitly as "the data infrastructure for trusted AI agents" ([Fivetran press](https://www.fivetran.com/press/fivetran-dbt-labs-complete-merger-to-create-the-data-infrastructure-for-trusted-ai-agents)). Interop is being standardized through the Open Semantic Interchange (announced Sept 23, 2025; ~25 vendors incl. Snowflake, Salesforce, dbt, Databricks; Apache-2 spec on GitHub) ([Snowflake press](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-salesforce-dbt-labs-and-more-revolutionize-data-readiness-for-ai-with-open-semantic-interchange-initiative/), [spec finalized](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/open-semantic-interchanges-specs-finalized/)).
5. **Semantics are moving into the database, and the layer is learning itself.** Semantic definitions are becoming warehouse-native DDL objects — Snowflake `CREATE SEMANTIC VIEW` ([docs](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/views-semantic/overview)), Databricks Unity Catalog metric views with `MEASURE()` ([GA post](https://www.databricks.com/blog/redefining-semantics-data-layer-future-bi-and-ai)) — inheriting warehouse RBAC for free. And the authoring bottleneck is being attacked with self-building semantics: Snowflake Semantic View Autopilot ([press](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-delivers-semantic-view-autopilot-as-the-foundation-for-trusted-scalable-enterprise-ready-AI/)), Databricks Genie Ontology — a "continuously learned" graph that weights definitions by source authority, usage, certification-proximity, and freshness ([June 16, 2026](https://www.databricks.com/blog/introducing-genie-one-genie-ontology-and-genie-agents)), Zenlytic's self-onboarding Zoë ([May 2026](https://www.prweb.com/releases/zenlytic-launches-zoe-self-learning-the-ai-data-analyst-that-onboards-itself-302773223.html)), Cube D3's "AI Data Engineer" ([June 2, 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cube-launches-d3-the-first-agentic-analytics-platform-built-on-a-universal-semantic-layer-302469860.html)).

**Our three sharpest differentiators (Synapse/Radix vs. the field):**

1. **Per-fact provenance with calibrated confidence tiers.** Nobody ships answer-level, per-fact evidence — sources + tier (grounded/inferred/guessed → Corroborated/Sourced/Reported/Inferred chips) with witness panels and conflict surfacing. The industry's granularity ceiling is the asset/query-level binary badge; even Databricks' Genie Ontology does multi-source authority weighting *internally* and never exposes it as user-facing evidence.
2. **Gated warehouse execution with hold-to-approve and an audit ledger.** Every commercial AI analyst auto-executes reads and offers explanation *afterwards*. Pre-execution machine gates (read-only shape → guardrail check → dry-run → byte/cost budget → row cap) + human sign-off + a per-attempt ledger exists nowhere as product; the closest things are generic MCP client tool-prompts and post-hoc payload logging (Databricks AI Gateway).
3. **The Brief: durable, claim-structured answer artifacts pinned to a content-addressed graph snapshot.** The industry's output is a chat transcript or a dashboard. Hex is closest ("work with the agent becomes a durable, debuggable asset", [Hex](https://hex.tech/blog/introducing-notebook-agent/)) but no one structures answers as claims-with-evidence, and no one offers reproducibility ("this answer was computed against snapshot `2026-07-01-ab12cd`").

**Our three biggest table-stakes gaps:**

1. **No verified-query escrow loop.** The single most standardized trust feature in the market (VQR / Trusted Assets / Verified Answers) is missing from our stack: stewards can't promote a validated Q→SQL pair into a repository that boosts future retrieval and stamps answers "verified." Our human-approval witness (weight 10) is the natural back-end for exactly this.
2. **No metric query engine / BI-consumable API.** MetricFlow, Cube, AtScale, Snowflake semantic views all *compile and execute* metric queries and serve JDBC/SQL/REST to BI tools; we emit validated SQL but have no `query_metric(metric, dims, filters)` execution path, no BI integration, and no OSI-format import/export — which is rapidly becoming the interchange lingua franca.
3. **No continuous-learning/bootstrap automation and no feedback capture.** The market auto-builds semantic models (Semantic View Autopilot, SpotterModel, Zoë Self-Learning, Cube's AI Data Engineer) and turns thumbs-down into instructions (Genie, Spotter coaching). Our graph compiles from witnesses but has no usage-driven refresh loop, no in-surface correction capture, and no eval-set UI (Genie ships benchmark sets in-product).

**Single best interface idea to steal:** Snowflake's **verified-query promotion loop** — Cortex Analyst retrieves human-verified Q→SQL pairs to ground new answers, marks matching answers as verified, and (since Snowflake Intelligence) *auto-surfaces candidate verified queries from live usage for steward review* ([VQR docs](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-analyst/verified-query-repository), [Snowflake Intelligence press](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-intelligence-brings-agentic-AI-to-the-enterprise/)). Grafted onto our Brief: "Promote this claim's SQL to verified" writes a `human_approval` fact (weight 10) and upgrades the evidence chip — closing the loop our SOURCE_WEIGHTS already prices.

## Ring 1 — Semantic Layer Platforms

### dbt Semantic Layer + MetricFlow (dbt Labs → Fivetran+dbt)

- **What it is:** metric definitions (semantic models, metrics, saved queries) written as **version-controlled YAML inside the dbt project**; MetricFlow compiles metric requests into SQL at query time. "Define business metrics once… and serve them consistently to every BI tool, notebook, and AI agent" ([dbt docs](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/use-dbt-semantic-layer/dbt-sl), [About MetricFlow](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/about-metricflow)).
- **Authoring/governance:** YAML in git — PR review, CI, environments; the same software-engineering workflow as dbt models. This is the archetype of "YAML-governed definitions."
- **Interfaces:** humans author in an IDE (dbt Cloud IDE/Fusion, any editor); consumers hit a **JDBC API (Arrow Flight SQL)** and a **GraphQL API**, plus first-party integrations (Hex, Mode, Tableau, Google Sheets/Excel) ([Semantic Layer APIs](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/use-dbt-semantic-layer/sl-faqs)). 2025 added a no-SQL query builder and an **"Analyst agent" in dbt Insights** — conversational analysis inside dbt's own UI ([2025 release notes](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/dbt-versions/2025-release-notes)).
- **2025–26 arc:** MetricFlow relicensed **Apache 2.0 and moved under the Open Semantic Interchange initiative** ([About MetricFlow](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/about-metricflow)); dbt MCP server launched Apr 21, 2025 ([launch post](https://docs.getdbt.com/blog/introducing-dbt-mcp-server)); remote MCP server GA + **dbt Agents** (governed task-specific agents) at Coalesce 2025 ([dbt blog](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/dbt-agents-remote-dbt-mcp-server-trusted-ai-for-analytics)); Fusion engine as the new compiler substrate ([dbt blog](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/dbt-labs-cost-optimization-agentic-ai-product-announcements)); **merger with Fivetran** (announced Oct 13, 2025, ~$600M combined ARR; completed June 1, 2026) explicitly framed as building "the data infrastructure for trusted AI agents," Handy: "trust is built at the infrastructure layer" ([Fivetran press](https://www.fivetran.com/press/fivetran-dbt-labs-complete-merger-to-create-the-data-infrastructure-for-trusted-ai-agents), [TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/searchdatamanagement/news/366643590/Fivetran-DBT-Labs-complete-merger-to-form-data-layer-for-AI)).
- **Reality check on adoption:** dbt's own 2025 State of Analytics Engineering (459 respondents): only ~30% use AI to consume data via natural language, and of those, **two-thirds do vanilla SQL generation vs. one-third through a semantic layer**; 27% plan increased semantic-layer investment ([summary](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/state-of-analytics-engineering-2025-summary)). The grounding gospel is ahead of actual practice.

### Cube / Cube Cloud → D3

- **What it is:** the self-described "universal semantic layer": data model in YAML/JS (cubes, views, measures, dimensions, joins, access rules) with a caching/pre-aggregation engine; Cube Core is Apache-2 OSS ([cube.dev](https://cube.dev/)).
- **Interfaces:** the widest API fan-out in the category — REST, GraphQL, **SQL API (Postgres wire protocol)** so BI tools connect as if to a database, plus an MCP server (OAuth-authenticated tenant endpoint) and AI APIs ([MCP docs](https://docs.cube.dev/docs/integrations/mcp-server)). Agents "form a structured request instead of writing SQL; the semantic layer compiles that request… with the user's access rules applied."
- **D3 (launched June 2, 2025):** "the first agentic analytics platform built on a universal semantic layer" — AI Data Analyst (NL → governed semantic SQL, charts, data apps) and **AI Data Engineer** (automates semantic model development and optimization) ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cube-launches-d3-the-first-agentic-analytics-platform-built-on-a-universal-semantic-layer-302469860.html), [Cube blog](https://cube.dev/blog/unleashing-agentic-analytics-with-cube-d3)). Trust UX: "explainable, auditable results with full lineage, access control, and certified queries"; "D3 doesn't just show you an insight. It shows you how it got there — the semantic SQL query, metric definition" ([DBTA](https://www.dbta.com/Editorial/News-Flashes/Cubes-D3-Platform-Reimagines-Analytics-with-an-AI-Agent-Native-Framework-169786.aspx)). Explanation is post-hoc; execution is automatic.

### AtScale

- **What it is:** enterprise semantic layer with an OLAP heritage; models compile to pushdown SQL, consumed by Excel (MDX!), Power BI (DAX), Tableau, JDBC — its moat is serving *legacy BI protocols* off one model ([atscale.com](https://www.atscale.com/use-cases/semantic-modeling/)).
- **Authoring:** GUI Design Center *and* **SML (Semantic Modeling Language)** — YAML, git/CI-friendly, **open-sourced Apache-2 in Sept 2024** with prebuilt industry models ([press](https://www.atscale.com/press/atscale-announces-the-open-source-release-of-semantic-modeling-language/), [GitHub semanticdatalayer/SML](https://github.com/semanticdatalayer/SML)).
- **AI story:** the loudest benchmark marketer. Its TPC-DS NL2SQL study: **92.5% accuracy with the semantic layer vs. 20% schema-only** across 40 questions ([Businesswire, Aug 2024](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240808651958/en/AtScale-Unveils-Breakthrough-in-Natural-Language-Processing-with-Semantic-Layer-and-Generative-AI), [blog](https://www.atscale.com/blog/semantic-layers-make-genai-more-accurate/)); also open-sourced a text-to-SQL leaderboard ([blog](https://www.atscale.com/blog/public-leaderboard-text-to-sql-tasks/)) and publishes "state of the semantic layer" retrospectives ([2025 review](https://www.atscale.com/blog/semantic-layer-2025-in-review/)).

### Looker / LookML (Google Cloud)

- **What it is:** the original governed modeling layer — LookML projects in git define dimensions/measures/explores; still the reference for "semantic model as code with PR review."
- **Interfaces:** Looker UI for humans; SQL Interface/API for tools; and since 2025 the **Conversational Analytics API** as "the agentic backend for Looker AI," embeddable in custom apps ([Google Cloud blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/understanding-lookers-conversational-analytics-api)).
- **Grounding stance:** Google's marketing centers on strictness: Conversational Analytics "does not guess how tables connect. It is strictly and inflexibly grounded by LookML" ([Google Cloud Medium](https://medium.com/google-cloud/architecting-trust-the-right-conversational-ai-for-the-right-data-layer-b95136fae600), [docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/conversational-analytics-overview)). GA came Nov 2025 with cross-Explore analysis (up to 5 Explores) ([GA post](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/business-intelligence/looker-conversational-analytics-now-ga)).

### Malloy (+ Publisher)

- Open-source **semantic modeling + query language** from Lloyd Tabb (LookML's inventor): measures, dimensions, relationships, and queries as first-class composable objects; authored in files with a VS Code extension ([Santa Cruz Works talk](https://www.santacruzworks.org/news/malloy-an-experimental-language-for-data), [Data Eng Podcast](https://www.dataengineeringpodcast.com/episodepage/malloy-hierarchical-data-semantic-models-and-the-future-of-analytics)).
- Explicit LLM positioning: defining analytical patterns once "minimizes the risk of errors and hallucinations." **Malloy Publisher** is "the open-source semantic model server… serves Malloy models through REST and MCP APIs, enabling consistent data access for applications, tools, and AI agents" ([GitHub](https://github.com/malloydata/publisher)).

### Boring Semantic Layer (BSL)

- Deliberately minimal OSS semantic layer **built on Ibis** (joint xorq-labs + Boring Data / Julien Hurault): semantic models as Python/YAML over Ibis tables, so one model compiles to DuckDB, Snowflake, or BigQuery SQL; **"MCP-friendly, perfect for connecting LLMs to structured data"** with an LLM-friendly JSON query format ([GitHub](https://github.com/boringdata/boring-semantic-layer), [launch post](https://juhache.substack.com/p/the-boring-semantic-layer), [BSL+MCP post](https://juhache.substack.com/p/boring-semantic-layer-mcp)). Signal: the "semantic layer as a Python library for agents" pattern is cheap to replicate — the moat is not the query compiler.

### Snowflake Semantic Views

- **What it is:** semantics as a **schema-level database object**: `CREATE SEMANTIC VIEW` with TABLES / RELATIONSHIPS / FACTS / DIMENSIONS / METRICS blocks, plus synonyms and comments; queryable via `SEMANTIC_VIEW()` in SQL (querying GA March 2026) and authorable via a Snowsight wizard ([docs](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/views-semantic/overview), [Snowsight UI docs](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/views-semantic/ui), [Snowflake engineering blog](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/native-semantic-views-ai-bi/)).
- **Why it matters:** definitions inherit Snowflake RBAC/masking/row policies natively, and they are *the* grounding primitive for Cortex Analyst, Cortex Agents, and Snowflake Intelligence. **Verified queries live inside the model** (see Ring 2). BI/notebook partners (Sigma, Hex, Omni et al.) read the same object.
- **Semantic View Autopilot** (2026): agentic auto-creation and *maintenance* of semantic views, "learning from live usage data and query history… automatically surfaces new verified queries for review" ([press](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-delivers-semantic-view-autopilot-as-the-foundation-for-trusted-scalable-enterprise-ready-AI/), [blog](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/semantic-view-autopilot/)).
- **Interop:** OSI founding member; dbt/Snowflake semantic interchange is an explicit OSI use case ([OSI press](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-salesforce-dbt-labs-and-more-revolutionize-data-readiness-for-ai-with-open-semantic-interchange-initiative/)).

### Databricks Unity Catalog Metric Views

- **What it is:** governed metric definitions in Unity Catalog — created in SQL/YAML, queried with the **`MEASURE()`** aggregate (no `SELECT *`), with display names, formats, and **synonyms "that help both humans and AI interpret"**; GA with full integration into AI/BI Dashboards and Genie; Databricks also announced **open-sourcing UC business semantics** ([GA blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/redefining-semantics-data-layer-future-bi-and-ai), [docs](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/business-semantics/metric-views)).
- Genie spaces built on metric views mean "every natural-language query Genie answers is grounded in governed, deterministic definitions" ([Databricks community](https://community.databricks.com/t5/community-articles/metric-views-in-databricks-a-unified-approach-to-business/td-p/139468)). Certification/endorsement of metric views rides UC's existing certification tags.

### Honeydew

- Snowflake-native semantic layer startup (Snowflake Ventures investment; Native App in Snowsight) with YAML/git + web UI authoring; serves Tableau/Power BI, Slack bots, and agents from one model ([honeydew.ai](https://honeydew.ai/), [Snowflake investment blog](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/investment-honeydew-business-intelligence/)).
- Its MCP server is the tell for where the category is going: agents can *explore models, edit models conversationally, and query* — "Honeydew compiles every MCP request into explainable steps and optimized SQL, preventing hallucinated joins, invalid filters, or mismatched metrics" ([MCP docs](https://honeydew.ai/docs/integration/mcp)); it also interops with native Snowflake semantic views ([blog](https://honeydew.ai/blog/honeydew-and-snowflake-semantic-views/)).

### GoodData, Kyligence, and the BI-giant semantic plays

- **GoodData:** long-standing logical data model (semantic layer since 2020) + headless APIs; **AI Assistant GA May 29, 2025**, RAG over the semantic layer; 2026 "Context Management" packages semantic model + governance + observability + knowledge grounding "into a single context layer" ([press](https://www.gooddata.com/press-releases/gooddata-rolls-out-ai-assistant-embeddable-generative-analytics/), [TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/searchbusinessanalytics/news/366640059/GoodDatas-Context-Management-aims-to-make-AI-trustworthy)).
- **Kyligence:** OLAP/metrics-store platform, grouped with AtScale/GoodData as "universal/headless semantic layers" in 2025 category roundups ([Coalesce.io playbook](https://coalesce.io/data-insights/semantic-layers-2025-catalog-owner-data-leader-playbook/)); low visibility in Western 2025–26 discourse.
- **Tableau Semantics / Tableau Next:** announced at TC25 (Apr 22, 2025) — "an AI-infused semantic layer… deeply integrated with Data 360" that grounds Tableau Next ("the world's first agentic analytics platform," per Salesforce) and Agentforce agents; GA with Tableau Next ([Salesforce press](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/tableau-next-announcement/), [tableau.com](https://www.tableau.com/products/tableau-next), [TC25 keynote recap](https://www.tableau.com/blog/top-10-announcements-keynote-tc25)).
- **Microsoft Fabric:** Power BI datasets renamed "semantic models"; the AI-readiness layer is "Prep data for AI" — AI data schemas, AI instructions, verified answers on the model ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/copilot-prepare-data-ai)).

**Ring 1 takeaway:** authoring has settled on *governed code* (YAML in git: dbt, Cube, SML, LookML, Honeydew, Wren MDL) with GUI wizards for the warehouse-native variants; consumption has settled on *SQL-protocol APIs + BI integrations + an MCP server*. The 2025–26 novelty is (a) semantics as database DDL and (b) semantics authored/maintained by agents, both of which collapse the standalone "metrics layer" category into platforms — exactly what the OSI standard and the M&A wave are responding to.

## Ring 2 — AI Analyst Interfaces on Semantic Layers

### Snowflake Cortex Analyst + Snowflake Intelligence

- **UX/architecture:** Cortex Analyst is a headless **REST API** (NL question → generated SQL + explanation) that developers embed; Snowflake Intelligence (GA **Nov 4, 2025**) is the end-user agentic chat app on top — "agents connect to semantic views… to deliver contextually rich, transparent and accurate responses"; 1,000+ customers had deployed 12,000+ agents at GA ([press](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-intelligence-brings-agentic-AI-to-the-enterprise/), [Cortex Analyst docs](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-analyst)).
- **Grounding:** semantic model YAML / semantic views (business names, synonyms, metrics, relationships) rather than raw schema.
- **Trust signals:** the **Verified Query Repository** — human-verified question→SQL pairs stored in the semantic model; Cortex Analyst retrieves semantically similar verified queries to ground generation, and matching answers are flagged as verified ([VQR docs](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-analyst/verified-query-repository)); Dec 2025 added agentic optimization of semantic views *from* verified queries ([release note](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/release-notes/2025/other/2025-12-02-cortex-analyst-optimization)).
- **Accuracy claims (vendor):** "delivers SQL accuracy of over 90% … nearly 2x as accurate as single-prompt SQL generation from GPT-4o and about 14% more accurate than other solutions"; on Snowflake's internal real-world BI eval, GPT-4o alone scored **51%** ([Evaluating Text-to-SQL Accuracy](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/cortex-analyst-text-to-sql-accuracy-bi/), [Behind the Scenes](https://www.snowflake.com/en/engineering-blog/snowflake-cortex-analyst-behind-the-scenes/)).
- **Execution model:** the API returns SQL; the calling application executes it under the caller's Snowflake role — governance is RBAC pass-through, not human approval.

### Databricks Genie → Genie One / Genie Ontology

- **UX:** Genie "spaces" — a curated scope (tables/metric views) + plain-text **instructions** + example SQL + sample questions; business users chat, get SQL + result + chart; thumbs-down feeds a review queue; authors maintain **benchmark/eval question sets** in-product ([set-up docs](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/set-up), [quality tuning](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/tune-quality)).
- **Trust signals:** **Trusted Assets** — parameterized example queries and UC functions vetted by a domain expert; "when a user submits a question that invokes a trusted asset, it's indicated in the response," i.e., an explicit *Trusted* label on the answer ([docs](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/trusted-assets)).
- **Genie One + Genie Ontology (June 16, 2026):** Genie One is the "agentic coworker" app; **Genie Ontology (preview) is the most important competitive artifact for us** — "a continuously learned graph" that extracts knowledge from tables, queries, dashboards, pipelines, and apps and, "using an approach similar to PageRank, weighs where a definition came from, the relative authority of that source's author, how often people rely on it, how closely it ties to certified and widely-used assets, and how fresh it is" ([Databricks blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/introducing-genie-one-genie-ontology-and-genie-agents), [typedef explainer](https://www.typedef.ai/blog/what-is-genie-ontology-databricks-continuously-learned-context-layer-explained), [CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/4186154/from-rag-to-ontology-databricks-bets-on-context-as-the-key-to-trusted-ai-agents-2.html)). Databricks claims Genie answered **84.5%** of eval questions right first-try vs. **52.4%** for the strongest general coding agent. This is multi-source fusion — but fused *inside the ranking*, never exposed as per-fact provenance to the user.

### ThoughtSpot Spotter

- **Approach:** not free text-to-SQL — questions compile to **relational search tokens** over a governed worksheet/model, and deterministic SQL is generated from tokens; ThoughtSpot's pitch: "if relational search tokens are correct, SQL is 100% accurate," with a **"Review business terms"** flow showing exactly how words mapped to tokens so users can verify the logic ([Spotter Semantics](https://www.thoughtspot.com/product/spotter-semantics), [getting started docs](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/10.8.1.cl/spotter-getting-started)).
- **Feedback:** in-context thumbs-down opens **Coach Spotter**, a curated coaching flow that fixes mappings for future questions ([docs](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotter-coach)).

### 2. Divergences and white space (the five specific checks)

1. **Per-fact provenance with calibrated confidence tiers — WHITE SPACE.** The market's maximum granularity is (a) an asset/query-level binary badge (verified/trusted/certified), (b) a citation link to a definition (Zenlytic) or grounding document (Power BI), or (c) colored flags on referenced objects (Alation TrustCheck). Nobody attaches *to each fact in an answer* the list of independent sources that support it, a calibrated tier, or conflict annotations. Genie Ontology computes source-authority weights but hides them inside ranking. No product distinguishes "corroborated by 3 independent systems" from "one LLM guess."
2. **Multi-source evidence fusion with explicit weights — EMERGING, OPAQUE.** Databricks Genie Ontology (June 2026, preview) is the first shipping fusion engine: definitions weighted by source origin, author authority, usage frequency, certification-proximity, freshness ([blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/introducing-genie-one-genie-ontology-and-genie-agents)). Select Star (query-log popularity) and Atlan (active metadata) fuse signals for *ranking/discovery*. But: no one fuses **profiling + query-corpus + curated expert knowledge + gated LLM enrichment** under declared trust priors, no one recomputes a per-fact tier on every touch, and no one exposes the fusion for audit ("explain_confidence" has no market equivalent). Expect fast-follow from Databricks/Snowflake; transparency of fusion is the defensible part, not fusion itself.
3. **Human-in-the-loop signing for agent-initiated warehouse queries + audit ledger — WHITE SPACE.** Universal industry behavior: reads auto-execute under RBAC; audit is post-hoc (AI Gateway payload logging, query history/tags). Human approval exists only as (a) the MCP client's generic tool-consent prompt, (b) curation-time approval (verified queries, glossary steward workflows — approval of *definitions*, never of *executions*), or (c) DIY patterns in blog posts. Nobody ships pre-execution machine gates (dry-run cost estimate → budget check → guardrail check) surfaced to a human as a signed hold-to-approve, with a per-attempt ledger. This is our most defensible mechanism, and it is precisely what regulated-industry buyers (banks) cannot get off the shelf.
4. **Durable answer artifacts vs. chat transcripts — MOSTLY WHITE SPACE.** Closest: Hex ("the work you do with the agent becomes a durable, debuggable asset" — but it's a notebook, not a claim-structure), TextQL's Ana emitting files, Cube D3's generated data apps, SpotterViz's generated Liveboards. All produce *documents or dashboards*; none produce **claim-structured answers where each claim carries evidence, tier, and a pinned snapshot version**, and none guarantee reproducibility of an answer against the exact context state that produced it. Content-addressed snapshot pinning appears nowhere in the market.
5. **Machine-enforced policy refusals as UX — WHITE SPACE.** Guardrails exist as *filters* (AI Gateway safety/PII/topic filters; Spotter "verifies the question… conforms to your administrators' established constraints"), and BI RLS silently narrows results. But a refusal that *names the violated rule, cites its provenance, and proposes the compliant alternative* ("this query LAG()s over roll_rate_calc, which guardrail G-12 forbids — here's the approved pattern") is not a product anywhere. Refusal-as-explanation is an unclaimed trust surface.

### 3. Positioning read for Synapse/Radix

**Where we genuinely differentiate:**

- **Provenance-typed knowledge graph with calibrated tiers.** Five-witness fusion (MDM, BQ profiling, analyst gold-SQL corpus, curated skills, gated LLM) with declared `SOURCE_WEIGHTS`, per-fact `Provenance` envelopes, and tier recomputation on every touch is *ahead of* Genie Ontology on transparency (they weight; we weight **and show**), and ahead of everyone on the corroboration principle (distinct-source breadth > single-source depth). The market's own benchmark discourse (data.world, Snowflake) validates the thesis; nobody has productized its *visible* form.
- **The 5-gate execution chain + hold-to-approve + audit ledger.** The market's execution governance is identity + quotas + post-hoc logs. Our chain (read-only shape gate → graph-guardrail gate → live dry-run → byte/cost budget → row-capped execute, every attempt in a ledger) with human sign-off is the only "agent never touches the warehouse un-witnessed" story, and it aligns exactly with what Readyset-style critics say is missing.
- **The Brief as the product.** Our blueprint's line — "the durable product is not the chat, it's the analysis artifact" — is validated by Hex's traction and by nobody having claim-level evidence chips (Corroborated/Sourced/Reported/Inferred) + witness panels + snapshot pinning. Chat is a commodity front-end (17+ products in Ring 2); the auditable artifact is not.
- **Guardrails as first-class queryable nodes** with machine enforcement (`validate_sql_plan`) and refusal UX — see white space #5.

**Table stakes we lack (buyers will assume these exist):**

1. **Verified-query escrow + badge loop** (VQR/Trusted Assets pattern) — steward-promoted Q→SQL pairs that ground retrieval and stamp answers. We have the write-path primitives (`human_approval` weight 10, curation queue) but not the loop or the badge.
2. **A metric compilation/execution surface for non-agent consumers** — JDBC/SQL-protocol or REST metric queries a BI tool can hit (dbt/Cube/AtScale/semantic views all have this), plus OSI import/export so our Metric contracts interoperate rather than compete with the emerging standard.
3. **Bootstrap + continuous learning + feedback capture** — auto-proposal of graph facts from usage (their Autopilot/Ontology equivalents; our loaders run on trigger, not continuously), an in-surface "that's wrong" → curation-queue affordance, and an in-product eval/benchmark set UI (Genie ships this; our golden-set harness is spec'd, not shipped). Also assumed: on-behalf-of user identity into BigQuery (we run a single SVC ID), and a Slack/Teams surface.

**Vocabulary to adopt / avoid:**

- *Adopt:* **"context layer"** (the category term of 2025-26 — but qualify ours as *provenance-typed* context), **"grounding"**, **"verified queries"** (use the industry term for our escrow when we build it), **"semantic model"** (for the OSI-compatible slice), **"trusted assets"**, **"on-behalf-of"**, **"MCP"**. Speak OSI fluently — it's where interop credibility lives.
- *Avoid:* "the first agentic analytics platform" (claimed three times in 2025 alone — Cube, Tableau, ThoughtSpot); absolutist accuracy claims ("100% accurate," "no hallucinations" — ThoughtSpot's token caveat shows how these claims get lawyered); bare "knowledge graph" as the headline (data.world proved the accuracy but the term didn't sell the company — lead with what it *does*: provable answers); "catalog" (we'd be judged against Atlan's UI depth); "copilot" (Microsoft owns it).

### 4. Steal / avoid list

**Steal (with source):**

1. **Verified-query promotion loop** — retrieval-grounding on human-verified Q→SQL pairs, "verified" stamp on matching answers, *auto-mined candidates from usage queued for steward review* ([Snowflake VQR](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-analyst/verified-query-repository), [Intelligence press](https://www.snowflake.com/en/news/press-releases/snowflake-intelligence-brings-agentic-AI-to-the-enterprise/)). Maps 1:1 onto our human-approval witness; a "Promote to verified" action on any Brief claim is the cheapest high-leverage feature we can add.
2. **Trusted-asset labeling on the answer itself** — the response says *which* vetted artifact produced it ([Genie trusted assets](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/trusted-assets)). Our analog: when a claim resolves via a skill's metric contract, badge it with the skill's identity, not just a tier.
3. **TrustCheck-style inline flags at SQL-authoring time** — endorse/warn/deprecate colors on every referenced object *while the query is being drafted* ([Alation](https://www.alation.com/docs/en/latest/welcome/BestPractices/UseTrustFlagstoProceedwithConfidence.html)). Our analog: witness-tier chips per referenced table/column in the SQL preview inside the Brief, before the approval gate.
4. **"Review business terms" token-mapping check** — show the user exactly how their words mapped to model entities and let them correct the mapping, not the SQL ([ThoughtSpot](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/10.8.1.cl/spotter-getting-started)). Perfect fit for our `disambiguate_term`/synonym machinery.
5. **Coach-from-failure flow** — thumbs-down opens a structured correction that persists as an instruction/example ([Coach Spotter](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotter-coach), [Genie](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/best-practices)). Our version writes a witness statement (correction event) so the graph, not a prompt, remembers.
6. **Session debug drawer** — a dedicated inspector (even a debug *agent*) attached to every AI conversation ([Omni Blobby](https://omni.co/demos/20250808)). Ours is stronger if it opens the witness panel + tool-call trace per claim.
7. **Deterministic-insight split** — statistics compute the facts, the LLM only narrates them, and the product says so ([Tableau Pulse insights platform](https://help.tableau.com/current/online/en-us/pulse_insights_platform_insight_types.htm)). Adopt the framing for our sandboxed computations: "numbers from code, words from the model."
8. **In-product eval sets** — curated benchmark questions with expected results, run on every space change ([Genie](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/tune-quality)). Ship our golden-set harness *inside* the steward console.
9. **Payload/inference logging as reusable data** — audit logs stored as queryable tables that double as eval corpora ([Databricks AI Gateway](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/ai-gateway/)). Our audit ledger should be a first-class queryable dataset, feeding the gold-SQL witness.
10. **Per-tool grants on the MCP server** — access to the server ≠ access to tools ([Snowflake managed MCP](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/managed-mcp-servers-secure-data-agents/)). Adopt for our MCP surface as we add riskier tools.
11. **Graph bootstrap agent with human review** — auto-proposed semantic objects that land in a review queue, not in production ([Semantic View Autopilot](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/semantic-view-autopilot/)); our curation pipeline already has the queue — add the proposer.

**Avoid (industry failure modes):**

1. **Chat transcript as the terminal product** — answers that evaporate; the market's own leaders are already routing around it (Hex artifacts, Pulse digests). We are right to make the Brief primary.
2. **Auto-modeling without review** — self-built semantics (Autopilot/Self-Learning class) shipped straight to production recreate the accuracy debt they claim to fix; keep human curation as the write gate.
3. **Binary "verified" as the only trust state** — it hides staleness and partial corroboration; keep tiers, add *recertification decay* (badges must expire — catalogs' stale-certification rot is a known failure).
4. **Unfalsifiable accuracy marketing** — "90%+" on private evals invites the lawyering AtScale/Snowflake numbers get; publish the golden set and methodology instead (AtScale's public leaderboard is the honest version).
5. **RBAC-equals-safety thinking for agents** — permission pass-through does nothing about prompt-injected exfiltration via tool chaining or runaway spend; gates + budgets + ledger are the answer (nobody else has them; that's the point).
6. **MCP tool sprawl** — exposing graph primitives instead of task-shaped verbs makes agents flail; our 15-verb, provenance-mandatory, loop-breaker design already follows the best practice the market is converging toward (dbt's tool families, Cube's structured requests).
7. **Grounding on raw schema "because it demos fast"** — the consistent 16–51% floor across every benchmark is the cautionary tale; never ship an ungrounded fallback silently.
8. **Betting against the platform bundlers** — Snowflake/Databricks will give semantic views + chat away "for free." Independent survival = cross-platform provenance + execution governance + artifact surface, i.e., the things the platforms structurally won't build first.
